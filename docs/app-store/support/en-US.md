## Create a plan and add an attachment

1. In **Plans**, tap **+**, choose a date, and add checklist items.
2. Select **Add attachment** in the plan editor to attach a photo, a PDF, or a link.
3. From another app, choose **Share → Countdown Manager**, select an existing plan, and save. Return to Countdown Manager to see the attachment in the plan.

## A reminder didn’t appear

Check the reminder time, repeat days, and time window. Check that notifications are enabled in Countdown Manager and in **iOS Settings → Notifications → Countdown Manager**. If you missed a sound or banner, also check your Focus and Scheduled Summary settings in iOS.

## Change the city or turn off weather

Open **Settings → City** and search for a city or allow the app to use your location. City search and forecasts require an internet connection. You can change **Show weather** in the app’s settings.

## Find the focus timer

Open a plan, go to its timer, and select a duration. With Live Activities enabled in iOS Settings, the active timer can appear on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island on supported iPhones.

## What happens to plans if I delete the app?

Plans and attachments are stored on your device. Choosing **Delete App** removes current local data. Check your device backup first if you want the option to restore that data. [About device backups](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108770)
