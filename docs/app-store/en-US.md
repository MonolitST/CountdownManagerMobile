## Description

```text
See how many days are left until your next trip, celebration, or deadline. Keep your checklist and the files you’ll need together in a plan with Countdown Manager.

• Count down to important dates. Create a plan for each event and mark key plans as favorites. Set a reminder for the day itself, a day ahead, or a week ahead.

• Get ready with a checklist. Track what’s done and attach photos, PDFs, and links to a plan or an individual checklist item. Use the share sheet in other apps to save an attachment to an existing plan.

• Set one-time and repeating reminders. Pick a time, or choose an interval, days of the week, and a time window. Mark a reminder done or snooze it for later.

• Make time to focus. Start a 5-, 15-, 25-, or 50-minute timer. Follow it on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island on supported iPhones when Live Activities are enabled.

Choose light or dark mode. Check the optional forecast for a city you choose or for your approximate location.

On iPad in landscape, open or edit a plan alongside your plan list.

Plans, reminders, and attachments are stored on your device. The app is available in English and Russian.
```
