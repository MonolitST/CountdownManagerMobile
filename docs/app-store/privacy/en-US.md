## Data the app uses

- **Plans and preferences.** Event names and dates, notes, checklists, reminders, focus timer data, your selected city, and preferences are stored on your device. Countdown Manager works without an account or a developer-operated sync service.
- **Attachments.** When you select a photo or PDF, the app saves a copy of the selected file. Links are stored with the plan and open when you choose them. The share extension uses the app’s shared local storage to add the items you select to a plan.
- **Notifications.** If you grant permission, the app gives iOS the time and text of each reminder for delivery on your device. Live Activities may show your active plan’s name and the remaining focus time on the Lock Screen.
- **Weather.** When you search for a city, the app sends the search text and language to Open-Meteo. It sends the selected city’s coordinates to get its forecast. If you choose to use your device location, the app rounds the coordinates to two decimal places, uses Apple’s geocoding services to identify the city, and sends the rounded coordinates to Open-Meteo for the forecast. [Open-Meteo privacy notice](https://open-meteo.com/en/terms) · [Apple Maps & Privacy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/apple-maps/)

## Storage and sharing

Countdown Manager keeps your plans, notes, checklists, reminders, and attachments off developer servers. The app uses no ads, advertising identifiers, or third-party analytics. If you open a saved link, the destination site handles your visit under its own policy.

Weather requests use HTTPS. Open-Meteo says it may keep technical logs with information such as IP addresses or coordinates for maintenance and troubleshooting, and that it deletes free-service logs after 90 days. [Open-Meteo privacy notice](https://open-meteo.com/en/terms)

App data stays on your device until you delete it. If you use device backups, Apple may include it in a backup. You can manage those backups through Apple’s settings. [What iCloud backs up](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108770)

## Your choices and data deletion

- To stop using device location, change the setting in **iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Countdown Manager**. You can choose a city manually. Turn off **Show weather** in the app to stop forecast requests.
- Manage notifications and Live Activities for Countdown Manager in iOS Settings. You can withdraw previously granted permissions there.
- Delete individual plans, reminders, or attachments in the app. To remove all current local app data, select **Delete App** in iOS. Copies in existing device backups are managed separately through Apple.
